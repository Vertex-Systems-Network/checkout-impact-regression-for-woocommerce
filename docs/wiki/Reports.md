# Reports

Click **Save report** after a comparison to keep it. The **Reports** tab lists saved reports per scenario with counts of changed, missing and needs-review fields; open one to see Before/After values.

Reports are deleted automatically after the retention period (default 30 days, 7–365 under Settings). A daily WordPress cron job removes expired reports in small batches. Scenarios and baselines are kept until you delete them.

With the [[Pro Add-on]] you can export a scenario's reports as CSV or JSON.
